Privacy Policy

App: pipeline-master-spike (Meta App ID 1515416390423526) · Last updated: October 21, 2026

pipeline-master-spike is a private test application operated by the developer of Pipeline Master, an automation engine. It is used only to test the official Instagram API (Instagram API with Instagram Login) with Instagram professional accounts that the developer controls or that were explicitly added as testers. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When an Instagram professional account owner connects the app and grants permissions (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_messages), the app may access:

  • Basic profile information of the connected account: account ID, username and account type.
  • Comments on the connected account's media: comment ID, text, commenter's Instagram-scoped ID and username, media ID.
  • Direct messages sent to the connected account: sender's Instagram-scoped ID, message ID, text and quick reply selections.
  • Access tokens issued by Instagram for the connected account.

How we use the data

  • To reply to comments and send direct messages on behalf of the connected account, as configured by its owner.
  • To test how webhook notifications and the messaging API behave.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, profiling or tracking, or share it with third parties.

Storage and retention

  • Webhook notifications are stored in server logs for the duration of testing and deleted when testing ends, at most 30 days after receipt.
  • Test fixtures derived from notifications are anonymized: IDs, usernames and message texts are replaced before storage.
  • Access tokens are kept encrypted or in files readable only by the operator, and are revoked when testing ends.

Data deletion

You can remove the app's access at any time in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Remove. To request deletion of any data related to your account or your messages, email vladis.tl.0880@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.
